# Long-term vision

**The end state we are building toward: every AI agent carries a credential, and no agent carries a password.**

## The five-year picture

Today, delegating to software means surrendering credentials: the agent becomes you, with everything that implies.
The world we want inverts that: authority is *issued*, bounded in math, and revocable in one gesture; identity stays
home. In that world:

- **Hiring an agent is as ordinary as installing an app**, and safer, because the permission sheet is enforced by
  proofs, not promises. "It can spend $30 on subscriptions until October" is a fact about the universe, not a
  policy.
- **Agent reputation is receipts, not reviews.** Track records accrue as cryptographic facts; a new agent economy
  gets its trust layer without inheriting the fake-review economy.
- **Merchants verify authority, not identity.** The checkout question becomes "is this action authorized by a live
  mandate?", which is answerable with zero personal data. Data-breach liability for agent commerce approaches zero because
  the data never existed at the merchant.
- **The rails interoperate.** AP2, ACP, and card-network agent mandates carry AgentPass receipts as their
  authorization evidence: the private layer under the public rails, the way TLS sits under the web.

## Why this is Midnight's flagship use case

Midnight's pitch is *prove facts, not data*: rational privacy that regulators can live with. Delegation is that
pitch with an economy attached: the facts being proven ("authorized, in scope, under cap") are exactly what
commerce needs, and the data being withheld (identities, budgets, habits) is exactly what regulation increasingly
forbids collecting. If the agent economy reaches even the conservative analyst forecasts, delegation proofs become
one of the highest-frequency ZK workloads anywhere, and every one of them is a Midnight transaction.

## What we refuse to become

No surveillance layer (we structurally cannot see caps, scopes, or identities, and intend to keep it that way
through every feature), no token, no walled garden: the credential format stays open so an agent's mandate and
receipts are portable across any directory that adopts the standard. If AgentPass succeeds, "Grant" is one storefront
among many on rails everyone shares, and that outcome is the success condition, not the failure mode.
